Large Market Virtual Meters

Downer's large market electricity sites are renewable under the FY26–28 renewal agreements. Envizi records that through a Certificates - Location - kWh account set up as a virtual meter of the site's large market electricity account. This page is the full brief and the working checklist: what to create, what to link it to, what to leave alone, and why. The first two are live and verified.

What this page is for

The renewal agreements that started on 1 July 2026 make every AU large market electricity site renewable (Category Management, 3 Sep 26: "all our large market sites except in the NT are renewable; none of our small market sites are"). For Envizi to show that, each site needs a certificate account that mirrors its electricity consumption — a virtual meter. This page lists every site, says what to do at each, and lets you tick the work off as you go. Progress is saved in this browser.

In one breath

  1. virtual accounts in scope — one per large market electricity account on a green register row, of which is already live and verified, leaving to create.
  2. register rows excluded as small market. Where Envizi styles the account Electricity Small Market, that classification is correct and the site is not under the renewal agreement.
  3. existing LGCS_ accounts to leave alone — checked 4 Sep 26, all hold 2025-and-earlier data, so none can become a virtual meter.
  4. WA Alinta accounts already renewable through the account's own green component since July — no certificate account needed. Plus 4 named exclusions: the three NT sites and QTMP.
  5. Set Opened On to 2026-07-01. That is what stops the meter reaching back before the renewal — of the sources have earlier data.

The rule that shapes everything

Envizi only lets an account be set up as a virtual meter while it holds no records. Two consequences:

  • The bulk Account Setup and Data Load (PM&C) template can't do this job — it can't create an account without a record, so each new account would be born non-empty. The accounts are created one by one in Envizi and linked there. The workbook's load tab stays as the record of what each should look like.
  • The old LGCS_ accounts, which carry the LGC surrenders from 2025 and earlier, can't be converted either. They stay as history and the new account sits beside them — which is why the new names are keyed on the electricity account (<account>_CERTS) rather than LGCS_<NMI>.

The way through both is the account's Opened On date. Set it to 2026-07-01 and the meter only mirrors the renewal period, leaving the source's earlier years — and the old LGCS_ record — untouched. That is exactly how the first two behave.

Fix the LGC emission factor first

The two live accounts use factor LGCs NSW 24-25 at −0.66 kg/kWh, while the electricity they offset uses 25-26 New South Wales at 0.64. A 100% meter therefore over-offsets by about 3% and the site total goes negative. Victoria has an LGCs Victoria 25-26 factor, so NSW simply needs its 25-26 equivalent added or mapped. Worth settling before the remaining are linked — 35 of them are NSW or ACT.

Why, and what the evidence says

The renewal agreements are LGC-backed

The rate schedules in Downer_Energy_Contracting_and_Budget_Summary_FY26-28.xlsx price the Engie and Origin contracts with an explicit LGC line — FY27 0.395 c/kWh (Engie) and 0.375 c/kWh (Origin), FY28 0.375 — plus a "renewable product $/yr". That covers 68 of the 78 green sites. The Alinta (WA) and Shell (TAS) contracts are modelled as an all-in delivered rate with no separate LGC line; Category Management confirms they are renewable, and the Alinta accounts already show 100% green kWh in Envizi.

What "green" means in the register

In the site register, green rows are renewable, light brown rows are not, and gas is unhighlighted. The 78 green AU Large Electricity rows are where the review starts; the 3 Northern Territory rows are the light-brown ones (Jacana, standing offer).

…and why 25 green rows still drop out

Only large market sites are under the renewal agreement. The register's "AU Large Electricity" is the portfolio group — how the tender was bundled — not the market classification. Where Envizi styles the account Electricity Small Market, that is correct, so the site is not under the agreement and gets no certificate account. Confirmed 4 Sep 26. That removes 25 rows, including every Queensland and Tasmanian row.

What the existing LGCS_ accounts are

69 accounts named LGCS_<NMI>, style Certificates - Location - kWh, supplier and reader LGCs, most created in one batch on 12 Mar 2024. They hold the LGC surrenders under the previous arrangement — data from 2025 and earlier, none for the renewal period. They are the right pattern (same style, same location, one per NMI) but the wrong vehicle for FY27 onward, because they aren't empty.

How the WA sites differ

Alinta's invoices carry the renewable share, and the Utilities Connector loads it into the electricity account's own Electricity - Green component: 100% of consumption, negative CO2e, from July 2026. That is already an offset in Envizi, so those seven get no separate certificate account. (SICEEP's Origin accounts show the same in June 2026 — the old contract — and nothing since, so they are in the create list.)

Step by step, per account

  1. Create it empty. Open the location, Add Account, style Certificates - Location - kWh, fill number / reference / supplier and Opened On 2026-07-01 from the card. Save without a record. (Copying the electricity account also works — a copy carries no data — that's where the Ecotricity accounts' "Copy of" prefix came from.)
  2. Link it. Open the new account, set it up as a virtual meter: source = the large market electricity account on the card, 100%, add. Link that one only.
  3. Verify. The new account shows kWh equal to the source for Jul and Aug 26, and the location's market-based CO2e drops to match. Tick Verified.

Reading the monthly data chips

Each card shows the source account's Jun, Jul and Aug 2026 electricity records from the Envizi summary. Actual = billed data loaded. Accrued = Envizi's estimate while the bill is outstanding; it's replaced when the bill loads, and the virtual meter will follow. No record = the account has nothing for that month (new accounts start in July). Where June shows data, the account pre-dates the renewal — that's the history flag.

Glossary

NMI
National Metering Identifier — the connection point. In Envizi it's the text after the last underscore in an account number.
LGC
Large-scale Generation Certificate: one per MWh of renewable generation. The retailer surrenders them to make a contract "100% renewable"; the rate schedules price this as c/kWh.
Virtual meter
An Envizi account whose records are calculated from one or more source accounts, at a percentage. Must be empty to set up.
Accrual
Envizi's estimated record for a period whose bill hasn't arrived, on account styles set to Contiguous Data. Certificate accounts are Event Data - No Accruals.
Green component
The Electricity - Green [kWh] part of an electricity account — the renewable share the retailer reports on the invoice.
