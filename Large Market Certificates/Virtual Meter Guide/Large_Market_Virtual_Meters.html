What this page is for

The renewal agreements that started on 1 July 2026 make Downer's contracted large market electricity renewable, backed by LGCs. For Envizi to show that, each site needs a Certificates - Location - kWh account set up as a virtual meter of the account carrying that site's electricity. This page is my working list: what to create, what to link it to, what to leave alone. Ticks and notes save in this browser.

How I scoped it

The green rows in the site register are the renewable ones (light brown is non-renewable, gas is unhighlighted) — 78 of the 81 AU Large Electricity rows, the other three being the NT sites on Jacana standing offer. Scope follows the supply agreement, so a green row is in unless it is a named exclusion or already offset another way.

The meter class does not decide scope

Category Management, 4 Sep 26, on a Mogo NMI I queried: "sometimes when you take both to a retailer they will agree to supply both as large market sites — that's probably what's occurred here… from a metering perspective it may be considered a small site, but from an electricity supply agreement perspective, it's being treated as a large site."

So where Envizi styles an account Electricity Small Market that is the meter classification, and it does not take the site out of the renewal. of the accounts I'm creating sit on a small-market-styled source for exactly this reason — every Queensland and Tasmanian one among them.

The LGC-backed evidence

The rate schedules in the contracting summary price the Engie and Origin contracts with an explicit LGC line — FY27 0.395 c/kWh (Engie) and 0.375 c/kWh (Origin), FY28 0.375 — plus a renewable product $/yr. Alinta (WA) and Shell (TAS) are priced as an all-in delivered rate with no separate LGC line; the Alinta accounts already carry 100% green kWh in Envizi, so those seven need nothing from me.

The rule that shapes everything

Envizi only lets an account be set up as a virtual meter while it holds no records. Two consequences:

  • The bulk Account Setup and Data Load (PM&C) template can't do this job — it can't create an account without a record, so each new account would be born non-empty. I create them one by one in Envizi and link them there; the workbook's load tab stays as the record of what each should look like.
  • The LGCS_ accounts carrying the LGC surrenders from 2025 and earlier can't be converted either. They stay as history and the new account sits beside them — which is why the new names key off the electricity account (<account>_CERTS) rather than LGCS_<NMI>.

The way through both is Opened On. Set it to 2026-07-01 and the meter only mirrors the renewal period, leaving the source's earlier years — and the old LGCS_ record — untouched. of the sources have pre-July data, so this matters on nearly all of them. It is exactly how the first two behave.

Fix the LGC emission factor first

The two live accounts use factor LGCs NSW 24-25 at −0.66 kg/kWh, while the electricity they offset uses 25-26 New South Wales at 0.64. A 100% meter therefore over-offsets by about 3% and the site total goes negative. Victoria has an LGCs Victoria 25-26 factor, so NSW just needs its 25-26 equivalent added or mapped. Worth settling before the remaining are linked.

Bathurst and Mogo went in on 4 Sep 26. This is what the export shows they are doing: the certificate account mirrors its source exactly, starts at July, and books the offset as negative CO2e.

What the check found

  • Mirroring is exact. Certificate kWh equals source kWh in every month, and it follows the source when a bill replaces an accrual — Bathurst's August moved from an accrued 49,402 to an actual 41,470 and the meter moved with it.
  • The date bound works. Both opened 2026-07-01 and carry no June record, even though both sources have June data.
  • The factor vintage is out. Certificates sit on the 24-25 NSW LGC factor against 25-26 electricity, so Bathurst nets −1.08 t in July instead of zero. See the callout above.
  • Mogo confirmed the scope rule. I built that one on 50002769514_4001127731, which Envizi styles small market, and queried it. Category Management came back that the site is supplied as large market — so the account is right where it is, and 34 more like it joined the list.

Per account, in order

  1. Create it empty. Open the location, Add Account, style Certificates - Location - kWh, fill number / reference / supplier and Opened On 2026-07-01 from the card. Save with no records.
  2. Link it. Open the new account, set it up as a virtual meter: source = the account on the card, 100%, add.
  3. Verify. The new account shows kWh equal to the source for Jul and Aug 26, and the location's market-based CO2e drops to match.

Glossary

NMI
National Metering Identifier — the connection point. In Envizi it's the text after the last underscore in an account number.
Large / small market
A metering classification, set by consumption (in NSW the large market threshold is 100,000 kWh a year). It is not the same as how the site is supplied, and it is the supply agreement that decides whether the renewal covers it.
LGC
Large-scale Generation Certificate: one per MWh of renewable generation. The retailer surrenders them to make a contract "100% renewable"; the rate schedules price this as c/kWh.
Virtual meter
An Envizi account whose records are calculated from one or more source accounts, at a percentage. Must be empty to set up.
Actual / accrued
The month chips on each card. Actual = billed data loaded; accrued = Envizi's estimate while the bill is outstanding, on account styles set to Contiguous Data. Certificate accounts are Event Data - No Accruals, so they carry no accrual of their own — they mirror whatever the source holds and follow it when a bill lands.
Green component
The Electricity - Green [kWh] part of an electricity account — the renewable share the retailer reports on the invoice.
